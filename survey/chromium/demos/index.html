Chromium survey: script-free demos

Small pages that show primitives the survey found (survey/chromium/README.md). None runs script. Open them in Chrome through the repo-root server (python3 -m http.server 8765 --bind 127.0.0.1 --directory <repo>).

  1. SVG child → parent: an SVG document in an <object> computes a number from its own state and the parent reads it with CSS. Chapter 01 §1.8
  2. The same, across processes: the child comes from another site, so it runs in its own renderer; open through localhost. Chapter 01 §1.8, §2.3
  3. Scroll-button counter: ::scroll-button on a 1 px snapport adds 1 per click; a scroll timeline reads it. Chapter 04 §1.8
  4. SMIL event latch: <set begin="on.click"> writes a class that :has() reads. Chapter 04 §1.9
  5. accesskey on a hidden checkbox: Alt+R toggles a display: none checkbox. Chapter 08 §1.6

Bug reproductions found during the survey are listed in bugs/README.md (files bugs/chrome-*.html).
