A child document sends a number to its parent

The teal box is an SVG document in an <object>. The parent animates its height (100, 200, 300, 50 px, one step a second). The child sets its own font-size: calc(50vh + 7px) and its outer <svg width="1em">, so it reports that as its natural width. The parent reads the width with anchor-size() and a size container and prints it with a counter. No script.

Expected readings: 57, 107, 157, 32, changing once a second. Cross-process version.
